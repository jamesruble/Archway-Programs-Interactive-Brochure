A Lifetime of Possibilities

Archway is Here Through Every Stage of Life

Archway Programs provides personalized education, early intervention, childcare, behavioral health, adult day services, employment opportunities, residential supports, and community-based programs that help children, adults, and families reach their fullest potential. Whether someone is taking their first developmental steps, entering school, building independence, pursuing employment, or seeking additional support, our services are designed to meet each individual where they are and help them achieve meaningful goals throughout life.

Early Childhood

Step By Step provides early intervention services for infants and toddlers experiencing developmental delays, helping children strengthen communication, motor, cognitive, and social-emotional skills during the earliest years of growth. Early Learning offers a nurturing preschool environment where young children develop school readiness, confidence, creativity, and a lifelong love of learning through individualized instruction and play-based experiences.

Childcare & Family Support

Just Kids provides safe, engaging before- and after-school childcare in 31 schools across 16 South Jersey school districts. Through enriching activities, homework support, recreation, and caring supervision, children have opportunities to learn, explore, build friendships, and develop confidence in a fun and supportive environment. The program also provides dependable care that gives working families peace of mind while their children continue to grow beyond the school day.

Specialized Education

Across Archway’s specialized schools, students receive individualized academics combined with behavioral support, speech, occupational and physical therapy, counseling, communication development, transition planning, and life-skills instruction. Every educational program is designed to help students achieve academic success, build independence, strengthen social-emotional skills, and prepare for meaningful opportunities beyond the classroom.

The Values That Guide Everything We Do

At Archway Programs, our core values guide everything we do. We are committed to Safety, ensuring every individual feels secure and supported; Compassion, treating others with kindness, empathy, and respect; and Individuality, celebrating each person’s unique strengths and empowering them to reach their fullest potential.

Creating Opportunities

From Creativity to Careers

Every individual deserves the opportunity to discover their talents, build confidence, and contribute to their community. Through Blue Violet and ArchWear, Archway creates meaningful pathways where creativity becomes purpose and skills become opportunities.

ArchWear provides custom apparel and promotional products while giving talented individuals meaningful opportunities to build workplace skills. Every project develops experience in production, customer service, order fulfillment, and retail operations, helping participants gain confidence, strengthen vocational abilities, and prepare for greater independence through meaningful employment experiences.

Together

Together, Blue Violet and ArchWear demonstrate how creativity can become confidence, meaningful employment, and greater independence.

Support Through Adulthood

Building Independence, Wellness and Community

Behavioral Health ACT Partial Care provides comprehensive behavioral health services that promote emotional wellness, recovery, personal growth, and community integration. Through individualized treatment, therapeutic programming, and skill development, participants are supported in building healthier, more independent lives.
Adult Day Services
The Advanced Abilities Day Program empowers adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities through individualized supports, community participation, recreation, volunteer opportunities, life-skills development, and meaningful daily experiences that foster confidence, independence, and personal fulfillment.
Employment & Community Engagement ArchWear and Blue Violet provide vocational training, employment experiences, creative expression, and retail opportunities where individuals develop workplace skills, gain hands-on experience, build confidence, and contribute through meaningful work in inclusive community settings.
Residential & Independent Living
Residential Services and Supportive Living provide person-centered supports that help adults live safely, develop daily living skills, strengthen relationships, participate in their communities, and achieve greater independence while maintaining dignity, choice, and quality of life.
